Sources: the live global top 200; the current top 1,000, built by merging every country's live top 200 and ranking by trophies; or the top 1,000 of the last finished Legend League season, ranked as that season ended. The two larger lists refresh hourly. Per-TH leaderboards are not in the API, so lower Town Halls may be empty. Armies come from ranked attacks in each player's battle log. An army's type is the troop filling the most housing space (Dragons and Dragon Riders count together; Healers never count), or Fireball when the Grand Warden carries it without a core troop such as Throwers. Balloons with a Lava or Ice Hound are LaLo. The 10 most used types are listed; the rest are under Other. Strategies within a type are patterns in what the armies carry (hero equipment, pets, spells, troops), since the API does not show how an attack was played. "Typical" lists what at least three quarters of a strategy's armies have and at most half of the type's other armies do. A variation is one set of main troops (5% of the army or more) with one set of heroes, equipment and pets; spells and small filler troops may differ.
